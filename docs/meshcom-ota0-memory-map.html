BLUF. The ota_0 image is 1,560,784 B on Heltec V3, 1,677,792 B on the classic T-Beam and 2,295,776 B on T-Deck Plus. On the 4 MB table (slot 3,403,776 B) the T-Beam is already at 99% of the raw staging limit (1,701,888 B = slot/2) before AU adds anything; Heltec V3 sits at 92%. With the AU TLS stack (+188 KB measured, -60 KB with trimmed root CAs = +128 KB net) Heltec V3 reaches 1,691,856 B, +10 KB against the raw limit; the T-Beam would be -104 KB (over). With the decided compressed staging (D11) both fit with 584 KB (Heltec) and 406 KB (T-Beam) to spare, so the de-bloat is about future headroom, not about AU fitting. T-Deck Plus uses 18% of a 12 MB slot and is not binding.

AU budget

What AU adds: the TLS stack (WiFiClientSecure + mbedtls TLS/x509 + certificate bundle) costs 188 KB (measured by the W0 probe as an image delta on Heltec V3, Measured). Replacing the 63.7 KB IDF bundle with the roots of the GitHub hosts saves about 60 KB (Estimated, AU-D12), so the net addition used below is +128 KB (131,072 B). Heltec V3 already carries 29.5 KB of mbedcrypto (WPA/SAE), T-Deck Plus 145.4 KB of mbedtls (WPA2-Enterprise pulls libmbedtls_2 and mbedx509), so the real T-Deck delta is smaller than 188 KB; it is not measured and the table stays conservative.

Classic vs S3 vs T-Deck

Same src/ (MeshCom src is 455 KB on Heltec, 456 KB on T-Beam), very different images. The T-Beam is 114 KB larger than Heltec V3 almost entirely in precompiled SDK blobs: Bluetooth controller libbtdm_app 123 KB vs 67 KB, libpp 56 KB vs 46 KB, libphy 45 KB vs 33 KB, IRAM text 122 KB vs 82 KB. No lever there; it makes the classic T-Beam the binding board. T-Deck Plus adds LVGL, audio decoders and TFT_eSPI (+429 KB), builds on the older platform espressif32 6.6.0 and links the full mbedtls TLS already.

Method and reproduction

  • Inputs: release builds at HEAD plus the uncommitted AU W1 work (a few hundred bytes of console code), copies of firmware.map / firmware.elf / firmware.bin for heltec_wifi_lora_32_V3, ttgo_tbeam, t_deck_plus. No rebuild, no hardware.
  • Image bytes = every output section stored in the flash image: .flash.text, .flash.rodata, .flash.appdesc, .iram0.text/.vectors and .dram0.data (loaded from flash), .rtc.*, classic .phyiram.*. RAM-only sections (.dram0.bss, *_noload) are excluded. The sum equals firmware.bin minus 114-174 B of image header and digest.
  • Linker-merged duplicate string literals are counted once (later duplicates only add the bytes beyond the highest address already seen in the section).
  • Reproduce: python3 tools/flash_map.py <map> --elf <elf> --label NAME --json out.json, compare two builds with tools/flash_map.py diff old.json new.json. Report data is in the JSON; this page is a hand-written analysis on top of it.
  • KB means 1,024 bytes throughout.
  • Compression numbers: zlib level 9 over the exact ELF bytes of the named input sections.
