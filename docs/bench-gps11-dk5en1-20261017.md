## False trigger at 16:23

Within ~30 s the raw fix stepped from 12 m to 37-43 m off the filtered position and stayed there for over a minute. The displacement detector (radius
clamp(3.5 x innovation rms, 40, 60) m held 15 s) declared moving at 16:22:59, the CV model followed the multipath, and
the 30 min average restarted at the excursion: the on-air position moved one cell for the rest of the run. The speed
path never fired.

## Decision and tests (2026-10-17)

The operator keeps the 40 m radius floor: one jump is acceptable against 119 raw cell changes in 2 h, and walkers whose
receiver reports speed 0 stay at 70-77 s. Two host tests pin the accepted behaviour:

- T9 `test_fixed_bench_dk5en1_mehrweg`: replays 15:50-16:45 of this capture
  (`test/test_pos_filter/bench_dk5en1_20261017.h`, 1099 fixes). At most one still -> moving, episode < 5 min, no
  reject or re-seed, output step < 15 m (measured: 1, 133 s, 0, 0, 8.8 m). With a 50 m floor: 0 triggers, 0.2 m.
- T5b `test_fixed_mehrweg_spruenge`: one burst per hour (30 s ramp to 25-45 m, 4 min hold, 2 min back) over calm
  correlated noise. Measured 6.5 false triggers per day on average (max 9), moving share 1.4 %, episodes < 190 s;
  bounds 10/day, 2 %, 10 min. With a 50 m floor: max 1/day.

Projection: this capture showed one such burst in 4 h. At that rate the model gives about 1-2 false triggers per day
at this site, each moving the on-air position by about one cell until the 30 min average settles back.
