# GPS-11 bench: DK5EN-1 stationary, 2026-10-17

Node DK5EN-1 (Heltec V3, external u-blox at 38400 baud, 10-11 satellites, HDOP 0.8-2.6), indoor/desk position with
strong multipath. Firmware `fork-dev` `a2381b97` (GPS-11 W2), flashed over USB. Captures over USB with
`tools/bench/serial_capture.py` and `--gpsdebug on`; one reboot at port open, before the first fix.

| FIXED | `--track off`             | 15:21 - 17:22 | 2407  | 3 s     | `tools/bench/runs/harness/gps11-w3-dk5en1-fixed.json` |
| TRACK | `--track on` (stationary) | 17:22 - 19:24 | 7202  | 1 s     | `tools/bench/runs/harness/gps11-w3-dk5en1-track.json` |

`--track off` restored afterwards and confirmed at the next boot. No reboot, no rejected sample in either run.

## Receiver

- Raw position: std 12-16 m, p95 26-31 m around the median. The fix drifts 30-40 m away over 10-20 min and back
  (correlation time of minutes, not the 90 s the host tests model).
- Speed while stationary: FIXED run p50 0.04, p99 0.26, max 0.44 m/s, 0 samples at or above 0.5 m/s; TRACK run (1 Hz)
  p50 0.08, p99 0.40, max 0.87 m/s, 19 of 7202 at or above 0.5 m/s.

## False trigger at 16:23

The raw fix drifted ~36 m from the filtered position and stayed there for minutes. The displacement detector (radius
clamp(3.5 x innovation rms, 40, 60) m held 15 s) declared moving at 16:22:59, the CV model followed the multipath, and
the 30 min average restarted at the excursion: the on-air position moved one cell for the rest of the run. The speed
path never fired.

## Offline replay and candidates

A host replay of the captured raw fixes through `pos_pipeline` (FIXED mode, real timestamps) reproduces the device
exactly (trigger 16:22:59, held 51.0 %, 7 cells, 11 changes). Candidates, replayed on both runs and checked against
`test_pos_filter`:

Without the false trigger the held output changes cell twice in 2 h; the share in the modal cell stays near 52 %
because the 30 min average itself crosses one cell border once. G1 as written (95 % in one cell) is not reachable for a
node whose true position sits near a border while the multipath bias drifts by metres over an hour.
