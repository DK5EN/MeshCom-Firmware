## Top corrections that change a verdict or a recommendation

1. Own-beacon starvation is not only strict priority. Dropped frames spent a median 20 % (own 29 %) of their life at
   the queue head without a TX, inside TX-free gaps of p50 40-44 s, because a prio 4/5 head needs a 5.5 s quiet gap
   that every received frame re-arms, and the age floor skips prio 4/5. The recommendation "per-class TTL (own longer or
   exempt)" alone does not get own beacons on air. Candidates for the operator: own-origin priority bump or extending
   the age floor to own prio 4/5 (inference, not tested).
2. CORE "CSMA_AGED 15709 events / 4100 episodes" is wrong: 13709 events, about 3670 episodes.
3. The counterfactual and longer-TTL numbers (+66-94 s, ~200 s stale, -79 %/-97 % drops) are model outputs; CORE
   states them as facts. "Exact for TTL <= current" is a first-order upper bound.
4. CSMA_AGED prio mix: prio 3 only 38 episodes (not ~140); XWJ's are prio 2.
5. Own HEY delivery: 29 %/61 % (of written) vs CORE's 42 %/79 % (of written minus superseded). Name the denominator.

Not checked: cf replay pick-match validation (99.0-100 %), stale air-age p50/p90 per node, CSMA quiet-gap replay
saving (0.6-1.6 s), relay refresh medians (707 s), "echo" percentages.
