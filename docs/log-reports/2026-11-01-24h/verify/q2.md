# Q2 TX slot pool: adversarial verification

Claims checked: `findings.md` and `results.json` for Q2, plus the Q2 block of CORE-FINDINGS. Measurements come from
`q2_checks/q2_verify.py`, which streams `norm/<node>.log` and does not use the analysis agent's scripts. Output:
`q2_checks/q2_verify_out.json`. Code was read at HEAD 651e0920. The field build is unknown, but the logs show
CSMA_AGED and RING_SUPERSEDE markers, so the age floor and supersede code are present in that build.

Method: each RING_WRITE is one entry. An entry ends at exactly one of these: RING_SUPERSEDE (old_id), RING_TX_READ
followed by `[LOG] TX x<id>` (aired), RING_TX_READ followed by `Destination-CallSign Error` (decode drop),
RING_DROP_STALE, or `[NBR]|CANCEL`. GW-ACK pairs share one msg_id; they are matched FIFO. Re-arm proxy: decoded
receptions (`[LOG] NNN ... RSSI:`). Do not use the "OnRxDone" lines for this: the handleACK early return
(lora_functions.cpp:1034) re-arms the hold but prints no "OnRxDone" line, so those lines undercount re-arms 2-3x.

## Top corrections that change a verdict or a recommendation

1. **The prio-4/5 cause needs a third factor (#4).** Strict priority blocks 64-72 % of a lost beacon's lifetime. In
   the remaining time, the 5.5-s hold (re-armed per frame, no floor) prevents almost every CAD attempt on OC
   (21 attempts in 2924 s). On IA the attempts are made, but 62 of 62 fail with CAD_BUSY. An age floor for prio 4/5
   alone would address only the head-time share, and on IA it would run into CAD_BUSY. A policy fix also needs
   aging into priority (or a per-class TTL for own beacons). The Handlungsbedarf verdict stands; the recommended fix
   should be widened.
2. **The IR ACK-wait figure is mislabelled (#6).** 18.8/31.2 s and 47 % are all prio-1 traffic, ping included. The
   real ACK wait is 20.9/31.7/60.5 s with 53 % >=20 s. The verdict is unchanged.
3. **The IR ping source is the node's `node_pingcall` setting, not a phone client (#8).** Validation belongs in
   enqueue or in the setting command. The verdict is unchanged.
4. **ACK wait at an empty queue is 16-18 s on IA/OC, not 12 s (#7).** This strengthens "hold, not queue depth". The
   22-24 s histogram step ties the release to the 20-s age floor.
5. **Minor (#5, #10, #12):** chains do go stale, 180 s after the last supersede. The slope range is -0.038..+0.014.
   The 45 is entries, not chains (28 chains).
