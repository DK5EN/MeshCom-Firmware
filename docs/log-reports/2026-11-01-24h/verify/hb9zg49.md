## zg.py review

- Group-to-relay mapping `g[i-1]` for ZG at path index i is correct.
- Bug: `groups()` drops non-3-field groups (`fullmatch \d+,\d+,-?\d+`). Older firmware (DB0SAC-12, HB9TVJ-12, HB9HEH-40, HB9HNO-49, HB9AK-2, F4AVI-12, DO3GB-1: 4.35x) appends 2-field `RSSI,SNR` groups; 2397 of 6927 HEY frames contain one. Dropping shifts all later indices. For ZG it has no effect (no 2-field group precedes ZG in any frame), but the "other relays" table is misattributed for frames where a 4.35 relay precedes.
- Dedup keys share one `seen` set ((mid,len), (mid,ZG), (mid,call)); no collision in practice. Fine.
- hb9vqq5.log contains no `[LOG]` lines, so the script yields nothing for it.

## New findings bearing on "deaf, not mute"

- ZG decodes HB9WAD-51 (TLORA, 22 km) at -111.8/-0.7 (25 samples over the week): ZG's receiver and RSSI chain do work for a signal that is ~equally strong as ZG at VQQ-2/VQQ-4 (WAD-51 -106/-14.9 and -118.6/-14.8 vs ZG -109.7/-16.6 and -119.6/-15.7). WAD-51 runs 4.35p and reports no rx, so the reverse is unknown. Not a refutation, but the receiver is not dead.
- The asymmetry is consistent across ~13 partners of different owners/hardware (12-20 dB vs 0-6 dB controls), which makes "every partner runs low TX power" unlikely, but TX power of ZG and partners is not observable in mcmap. Conclusion stands as "ZG's loudness-minus-hearing is 10-15 dB above its neighbours"; deaf vs over-loud needs `--info` from HB9JAY (TXPWR, RXBOOST).
