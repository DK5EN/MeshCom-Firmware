SUPERSEDED 2026-09-12 -- DO NOT USE THESE NUMBERS.

Kept only as the record of a measurement that was wrong in two ways, because
docs/w3-settings-verdict.md refers to it:

  1. HOST ABI, not the device. This was compiled for macOS arm64, where
     `unsigned long` is 8 bytes; on the nRF52 it is 4. The struct is 2000 B on
     hardware, NOT the 2008 B below, and 15 of the 18 region boundaries listed
     here do not exist on the device at all.

  2. WRONG INSTRUMENT. Per-byte schema coverage was the gate proposed after the
     RAK-90 settings loss. The Fable review (verdict Finding 5) refuted it: it
     invents a padding-classification problem, is per-build-env rather than
     per-platform, cannot see a field covered by a WRONG-TYPED descriptor, and
     its clean state would read 36 % red. Of the 724 bytes it flags, zero are a
     defect -- they are flash markers, the device EUI (rebuilt each boot),
     deliberate exclusions, and the struct's own "nicht im Flash" section.

The replacement invariant is member-level and fail-closed, described in the
verdict. The "uncovered" figure below never justified adding a single field.
